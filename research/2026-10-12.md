## Buyer, alternatives, and commercial hypothesis

- **Buyer:** a small Sri Lankan social-commerce merchant with repetitive WhatsApp/Instagram order entry.
- **Pain hypothesis:** time spent transcribing SKUs, wrong quantities, missed items, duplicates, and inconsistent manual approval. No interviews have confirmed prevalence or urgency.
- **Alternatives:** free WhatsApp Business plus spreadsheets/POS, Wati, Manychat, respond.io. Differentiation to test is simpler approval-first capture, local catalog totals, durable deduplication, and affordable onboarding; no moat demonstrated.
- **Distribution:** five opt-in merchant interviews and two private offline demos with synthetic/redacted data. No outreach performed.
- **Price experiment:** LKR 2,500–7,500/month and optional onboarding fee. These are guesses, not willingness-to-pay evidence. A bespoke integration service may be a faster first sale, but support time matters.
- **Costs:** local Python/SQLite demo uses no paid APIs or hosting. Production costs unknown: hosting, backups, authentication, maintenance, customer support, inference, provider subscriptions, message fees, taxes. Gross margin cannot yet be estimated credibly.

## Validation gate and next engineering step

Interview five merchants with permission: orders/day, manual minutes/order, errors/week, current tools, biggest pain, desired workflow, and willingness to trial at a concrete price. Use the anonymous CSV template in this directory, and never commit personal/customer data. Time two supervised private demos. Provisional go/no-go gate: at least two merchants want a pilot and at least one discusses a specific paid price; this threshold is a proposed decision rule, not evidence.

Engineering: 28 local Python unittest tests passed (12 prior + 16 new) on Python 3.13.5; CLI smoke test succeeded. SQLite queue persists only parsed drafts, source IDs, hashes, notes and timestamps; no raw message text. One-way review status is bookkeeping only; it cannot fulfill or send orders. Remaining gaps: no user authentication, data retention/deletion controls, encrypted storage, production backup, multi-user isolation, free-text AI extraction, messaging integration, or actual customer feedback. Next after interviews: local review/correction UI with auditability.
