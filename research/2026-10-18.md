# Daily Innovation Lab — 2026-10-18

## Outcome and investment decision

Continue the **offline, approval-first OrderPilot** experiment, but **do not build paid autonomous messaging yet**. The new `unit_economics.py` and `scenario_compare.py` allow prospective merchants to compare lower-cost, human-reviewed workflow assumptions against a paid WhatsApp Business Platform scenario. All model prices are hypothetical LKR inputs, and no customers have been contacted.

Over 70% of exporters and 75% of importers reported using e-commerce; 67% of e-commerce firms reported cybersecurity risk exposure. This is evidence for digital-commerce activity and security concerns, but is **not representative** of the small domestic social-commerce merchants we propose to serve. https://odi.org/en/publications/e-commerce-and-international-trade-in-sri-lanka-findings-from-a-survey-of-953-exporters-and-importers/

4. **OWASP AI Agent Security Cheat Sheet, checked October 18, 2026** advises explicit approval for high-impact actions, least privilege, and auditable decisions. OrderPilot intentionally has no autonomous outbound messages, payments, or fulfillment. https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/AI_Agent_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html

## Commercial assessment — hypotheses vs observed evidence

| Dimension | Working hypothesis / verification needed |
|---|---|
| Customer | Small Sri Lankan online retailer receiving repeat SKU orders by chat, 10–40 orders/day; not yet interviewed |
| Urgent pain | Re-entering chat orders, wrong quantities, duplicates, and time lost on verification; not yet measured |
| Buyer | Business owner or operations manager; budget authority must be confirmed |
| Alternatives | Free WhatsApp Business app plus spreadsheet, existing POS, Wati, respond.io and other inbox automation products |
| Differentiation | Low-cost offline review, correction audit trail, no mandatory API messaging, explicit approval and transparent unit economics; no demonstrated moat |
| Distribution | Consent-based 5-merchant interview pilot, then private demonstration and referrals; no outreach performed |
| Price experiment | Ask about LKR 2,500 / 3,500 / 5,000 / 7,500 per month as distinct options, not market-validated prices |
| Operational costs | Hosting, support, backups, AI inference if added, paid API subscription, per-message charges, currency conversion, taxes |
| Feasibility | Offline Python/SQLite demonstrator works; authentication, backups, data retention, encryption and public deployment remain incomplete |

### Synthetic sensitivity example

Assumptions: 62 seconds saved/order, LKR 400/hour labor value, LKR 3,500 monthly subscription, two service messages/order at **assumed** LKR 3 each, one utility/order at **assumed** LKR 4, with 1,000 free service messages monthly.

| Monthly orders | No-paid-messaging modeled net LKR | Paid Platform modeled net LKR |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | -2,811.11 | -3,211.11 |
| 300 | -1,433.33 | -2,633.33 |
| 520 | +82.22 | -2,117.78 |
| 1,000 | +3,388.89 | -3,611.11 |

**These figures are fabricated scenario inputs, not evidence of realized savings, provider charges, merchant income, or customer willingness to pay.** The two workflows are assumed to save the same labor time solely for isolating messaging costs; real measurements could invalidate this assumption.

## Falsifiable customer-validation gate

- Conduct **five opt-in merchant interviews** outside this autonomous run. Ask for order volume, error frequency, existing tools and *a named price they would seriously consider paying*.
- With permission, measure at least **20 paired numeric-only tasks**. Remove customer identifiers and raw messages. Use `orderpilot.paired_study` for descriptive statistics.
- Test a **no-paid-message** workflow first; avoid provider contracts and costs until expected value and consent are established.
- Decision: continue building only if two merchants demonstrate recurring pain and one explicitly discusses a paid pilot. This is a founder screening heuristic, not statistical proof of market size or demand.
- If results are weak, consider **invoice exception review** or **merchant reconciliation** instead, but do not pivot without credible alternative buyer evidence.
