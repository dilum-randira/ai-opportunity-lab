# AI Opportunity Lab — 2026-10-13

## Commercial research: OrderPilot's first paying customer

**Decision:** Continue the approval-first OrderPilot prototype, but validate unit economics before adding expensive messaging or AI inference. Buyer hypothesis: small Sri Lankan merchants who manually transcribe orders from social messages into spreadsheets or POS. Alternatives include WhatsApp Business + spreadsheets, Wati, Manychat, and respond.io. Potential differentiation: simple SKU validation, durable duplicate detection, merchant approval, and transparent costs. These are hypotheses, not verified market demand.

## New offline engineering experiment

A privacy-minimal Python time-study and monthly ROI simulator was implemented and **25 local tests passed** (12 original parser tests and 13 new simulator tests). It reads four numeric columns only: baseline_seconds, assisted_seconds, baseline_errors, assisted_errors. It reports mean/median time saved, deterministic paired bootstrap interval, subscription/setup/hosting costs, configurable messaging costs, net monthly value, and conservative break-even order count. The simulator is not a customer-facing product or AI agent; publication of the implementation to GitHub is subject to repository tool permissions and must be verified separately.

**Synthetic example:** 10 fake paired observations suggest mean 62 seconds saved/order (sample bootstrap interval 52.50–73.00 seconds). At 520 orders/month and assumed LKR 400/hour labor value, labor savings are LKR 3,582.22. Add a hypothetical LKR 3,500 subscription, 2 service messages/order at **invented** LKR 3 each, and 1 utility template/order at **invented** LKR 4, with 1,000 free service messages: messaging costs LKR 2,200, total costs LKR 5,700, and net value is **negative LKR 2,117.78**. These are not Meta's official Sri Lankan rates or measured merchant outcomes. The negative case argues against messaging-heavy automation until a buyer confirms sufficient savings.

## Validation gate

Interview five consenting merchants, measure 20+ paired tasks using synthetic or redacted order data, confirm current local provider and platform fees, and ask at least one merchant to discuss a specific paid pilot. Hypothetical pricing to test: LKR 2,500–7,500/month and optional setup. No customers contacted, payments made, costs incurred, or deployments performed. Next engineering step: audited manual correction of ambiguous drafts, followed by a local review UI.

**Uncertainty:** No verified customers, demand, labor savings, production unit economics, or revenue yet. Small paired samples cannot establish causal effects.
